- T6 (2026-08-26): Batch 6 of the dengue-forecasting plan — the vertical slice, `done — produced`. A probabilistic persistence baseline implemented as a Chap `MLproject` with a `uv_env`, run through `chap eval` on the development file at batch 3's scheme: **mean CRPS 24.879, MAE 29.073, coverage 0.666 (10–90) and 0.491 (25–75), over 371 cells, 16 provinces, 8 splits, in 16 seconds**. Every link in the chain — archived data → development file → model contract → `chap eval` → chap-core's own metric → the contract files batch 5's design specifies — has now been exercised once with a file at every join. **The corridor is bounded on both sides**: untuned regression 44.055 (batch 4), this baseline 24.879, reference 21.891, so a candidate has about 3 CRPS of room. **The two models are miscalibrated in opposite directions** — the reference's central interval is much too wide (0.617 against 0.50) while this one's is nearly exact (0.491) and its tails are much too thin (0.666 against 0.80) — and per-province 10–90 coverage runs from 0.042 in Salavan to 1.000 in four provinces, the first measurement of what the unweighted mean over sixteen unequal provinces hides. **A "trivial" baseline turned out to contain a real judgment call**: two published constructions exist for putting a predictive distribution around a persistence forecast and they disagree; the non-parametric empirical-change form was taken because the parametric one's floor on the mean would set the distribution's width in the majority of cells that are zero, and the choice is now a fork in the plan's inventory. **Rule 3 for models closed**: the model ships exact pins and an interpreter pinned to the patch, and its `uv.lock` was verified byte-identical to the one chap-core's runner actually used, so a model's environment is pinned by a tracked file rather than re-resolved at run time. **Rule 6 satisfied by construction**: the model has no randomness — draws are the empirical quantile function at fixed levels, not samples — verified by two independent runs coming back byte-identical. Not done, deliberately: the paired per-cell comparison against the reference, which needs both models scored from inside the tree and is batch 7's. Report: `26-08-26_b06_verticalSlice.md`. `/validate invariants` passes.
- T7 (2026-08-26): Batch 7 of the dengue-forecasting plan — erecting the claim tree, `done — produced`, and phase B closes. Built twenty nodes from batch 5's design with `/node`: `02_setup` (four forks in sequence, each handing the dataset on, with an assembly step that gives every model one dataset and one set of evaluation flags), `03_models` (two baselines and the external reference), `04_score` (per-cell collection, a weighting fork, the leaderboard and the paired comparison), and the root's `conclude.py`. **`analysis/run.sh` now reproduces the whole reported analysis end to end in about twenty minutes**, eighteen of them the emulated reference. The persistence model moved into its node with `git mv` and reproduced batch 6's mean CRPS to the last digit (24.879338288409706), which is the check that the tree added no arithmetic of its own. Seasonal climatology added as the second required baseline: **24.337**. The reference scored from inside the tree for the first time, four repeats, per-cell mean **22.098** (individually 21.820–22.385). **The batch's substantive finding is what the comparison can resolve.** Pairing cell by cell is two to four times tighter than batch 4's unpaired 5.68 — standard error 1.34 to 2.05 treating the 371 cells as independent, 1.92 to 2.99 clustered by split — but both baselines sit 2.2 to 2.8 CRPS from the reference, so **neither difference clears two standard errors on any reading**. The development backtest resolves roughly **4 CRPS**, wider than the whole corridor from persistence to the reference, and below **0.57 CRPS** nothing can be attributed to a model at all, that being how far the unseeded reference moves against its own repeats. A candidate can top the leaderboard without the evaluation being able to say it beat the reference, and that is now measured rather than feared. Two further findings phase C needs: **at one month's lead a persistence baseline is level with the reference** (16.43 against 16.54), which earns its aggregate advantage entirely at two and three months; and **the reference is beaten by both baselines in the two provinces carrying the most evaluated cases** while winning nearly everywhere else, so the cheapest fork in the project — how the headline mean is weighted — may also be one of the most consequential. Fixed at the cause; the installer now installs from the lockfile and reports any difference. Five smaller corrections, each found by a run or a check.
