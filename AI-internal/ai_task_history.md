- T8 (2026-08-27): Batch 8 of the dengue-forecasting plan — the candidate contract and candidate 1, `done — produced`, and phase C opens. Built `03_models/03_candidate` with `a_hierNB` and one main-path child under each of its four configuration forks (`01_observation/a_negBinomial`, `02_covariates/a_lagged`, `03_population/a_offset`, `04_fitTime/a_trainOnly`), each fork writing an option specification that the node's own assembler merges into the one configuration file `chap eval` is pointed at. **This settles how model configuration reaches an `MLproject` model**, open since batch 2 and unexercised because neither baseline has any: `--model-configuration-yaml` → `ModelConfiguration` → `model_configuration_for_run.yaml` in the run directory → the `{model_config}` placeholder in the entry points, so a run's configuration is a stored artifact rather than a command line. Confirmed against `chap-models/ewars_plus_template`, which batch 5 flagged and left unread and which also supplied the default covariate pair and lag — its published `laos_eval_config.yaml` names rainfall and mean temperature at `n_lags: 2`. **Rule 6 gets its first real surface**: `analysis/scripts/lib/project_seed.py` reads the one project seed from the settings table that declares it and derives a component seed by BLAKE2b, and the derived seed travels into the model inside the configuration file; verified by two runs and a diff, identical. The model is a hierarchical negative-binomial GLM on three pinned dependencies — population offset, two-harmonic shared season, lagged standardised climate, and province and province-year effects pooled toward a common level — fitted by Fisher scoring with EM updates for the variance components, drawing forecasts from the Laplace posterior, from the prior on the year effect, and from the negative binomial on top. Two seconds to fit, 43 seconds for the eight-split backtest. **It scores mean CRPS 26.100 — last of the four models, worse than both required baselines — and mean absolute error 27.106, the best in the project, ahead of the reference's 28.902.** The centre is right and the width is wrong, and wrong locally rather than on average: two provinces carry 2.4 of the 4.0 CRPS gap to the reference, with the 10–90 interval covering every outcome in Vientiane Capital and an eighth of them in Salavan, because the province-year variance is one number shared by all provinces and so a constant multiplicative width. The aggregate coverage of 0.720 sits between two failures and describes neither. **A result that revises batch 7**: the backtest's resolution depends on the pair being compared, not on the dataset alone — the candidate's per-cell differences against the reference are half as variable as persistence's (clustered standard error 1.11 against 2.99) because the two models are structurally alike and fail on the same cells, so this is the first comparison in the project that clears two standard errors, and what it says is that our candidate is worse. `conclusion.json` now reports `candidate_exists: true` and the headline skill score moved from −0.101 to −0.181, which is the machinery working rather than a regression. Deliberately not done: an autoregressive term on lagged counts, which no fork covers and which is proposed to batch 9 as a fifth fork rather than added silently; and re-running the unseeded reference, which would have replaced batch 7's four repeats with a different draw and moved the denominator of every conclusion. **`check_invariants.py` changed and that is a change to the method**: its `crossing` heuristic used a whitespace class before a trailing comment and `\s` matches newlines, so a modelling constant followed by a blank line and a paragraph of prose was reported as a transcribed value; the separator is now spaces and tabs, which is what the docstring's "trailing comment" means, and nothing that was a finding stops being one. Report: `26-08-27_b08_candidateContract.md`. `/validate invariants` passes.

Skill score −0.181 → −0.072. **The batch's more useful finding is methodological: one-at-a-time fork effects do not add.** A second sweep around the promoted main path re-measures every child — the hurdle is worth 0.601 there rather than 2.115, the per-province annual variance 0.051 rather than 1.870, and dropping the climate covariates changes sign — so three forks worth 4.632 CRPS separately delivered 2.402 together and two of nine children reversed sign. Tier 1 of the phase-D manifest is a one-at-a-time sweep and therefore measures a quantity that does not compose; **tier 2's pairs, which were first in line to be cut, are now the part of the manifest with evidence behind it**. Half of batch 8's width diagnosis holds: Vientiane Capital's coverage came off 1.000 to 0.88 and the candidate now beats the reference there, while Salavan's stayed at 0.12 — a per-province variance widens a province whose variance is genuinely large and does nothing for one whose epidemic years are sharper than any log-scale variance can represent. **Two decisions left visible rather than taken**: the promotion rule was applied once and not iterated to a fixpoint, although two children (refitting inside `predict`, +0.873; the richer covariate set, +0.821) are outside the floor from where the model now stands and would take the candidate to about 22.8 — iterating is greedy coordinate descent on development CRPS, which is the failure phase C warns about and which one held-out year cannot diagnose, and the cost of stopping is stated rather than hidden. **Kept in the record**: the promoted fit runs to its 200-round cap because the convergence test is a maximum over seventeen relative movements and the smallest variances keep it above tolerance long after the parameters have settled — the tolerance was **not** relaxed; a `fit_time = predict` combination leaves no fitted object, since the fit happens once per split inside chap-core's untracked run directories; and the autoregressive premise script first computed correlations of `NaN` by reading `1998-01` as `199801`, fixed at the source and re-run. - T21 (2026-08-27): Batch 21 of the dengue-forecasting plan — the greedy branch, `done — produced`, **on a git branch named `greedy` that is never merged**. The number follows the batch, not the sequence: batches 10–20 are still open, and this one was taken out of order because the human asked what iterating batch 9's promotion rule would have produced. Batch 9 applied that rule once and stopped, on the grounds that iterating is greedy coordinate descent on development CRPS; the human settled it both ways — the main path does not iterate, and the iterated path runs on a branch so that what stopping cost is measured rather than estimated. `AI-internal/useful-scripts/greedy_iterate.py` executes the rule rather than leaving it to a reading of a table: each round writes down what every fork's best child scored and which forks it moves, *before* it touches the tree. The rule is batch 9's with the "applied once" clause removed and a round cap of 8 added, and it was committed before the first round it decided was run. **Three rounds, then a fixpoint.** Round 1 moved `02_covariates` → `b_rich` and `04_fitTime` → `b_refitAtPredict`, 23.698 → **21.857**; round 2 moved `05_autoregressive` → `b_lag3`, → **21.275**; round 3 swept every fork again and found the best remaining move worth **0.150** CRPS, a quarter of the resolvable floor, so the branch stopped because the rule ran out and not because the cap bound. **The greedy candidate beats the reference model — 21.275 against 22.098, and against each of its four repeats individually — with a skill score of +0.037 where the main line's is −0.072, MAE 26.278, and 10–90 coverage 0.741 against the main line's 0.701.** By lead time it is 17.97 / 20.41 / 25.44 against the reference's 16.54 / 21.97 / 27.79, so it wins at two and three months and loses at one, where the whole remaining gap is. **And it changes nothing the project can conclude**: the paired per-cell difference is −0.823 CRPS with a split-clustered standard error of 1.602 — half a standard error, where batch 9's candidate was 1.03 on the other side, and 50.4 % of cells. The main line's "we cannot separate these two" survives the counterfactual with the sign of the point estimate reversed, which is the strongest available evidence that stopping cost the project nothing it reports. **What iterating cost is paid in the record, not in the score.** The first fork the rule moved is `04_fitTime` → `b_refitAtPredict`, and under `fit_time = predict` the fit happens once per split inside chap-core's untracked run directories: the branch's `fitted_model.json` is a **520-byte stub** where the main line's carries seventeen annual variances, two blocks of coefficients and an EM history. A rule that selects on development CRPS cannot see whether the model it selects can be inspected, and that is an argument for the main line's decision that the score alone does not give. **A second demonstration that one-at-a-time fork effects do not compose, with the sign reversed:** the lagged-count term is worth −0.075 around batch 8's configuration, +0.353 around batch 9's, and +0.582 once the model refits inside `predict` — it and the refit are complements, and no one-at-a-time sweep can see a complement. With batch 9's overstatement finding, **tier 2 of the phase-D manifest now has two independent demonstrations behind it and batch 12 must not cut it**. Salavan is untouched by two more rounds of selection (47.4 against the reference's 38.4, coverage 0.21), so the remaining gap is where batch 9 said it was. **Kept in the record**: the loop ran unattended between commits, so the tree's main-path markers moved twice inside one commit and round 2's per-combination results were replaced by round 3 before any commit held them — batch 9's hand-run rounds are recoverable at `49825b5` and these are not; and **`verify_model_determinism.sh` has reported `status: differs` since `models.csv` gained its `scored_under_combo` column**, because the check compares that column across two scratch combinations whose names differ by construction — the per-cell scores and fitted objects match in every case, which is what batch 9's report claims in words, but its parenthetical citation of the file's status (and T9's above) is wrong. Nothing was changed: the cause is in the check's specification, not in the models, and the repair is the human's.
